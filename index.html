Privacy Policy

Your money.
Your data.

Flow is built on a simple principle — your financial data belongs to you and only you. This policy explains exactly what we collect, what we don't, and how your information is handled.

Effective July 1, 2025 Offline-first app No ads, no tracking
01

Overview

Flow ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal finance management application. We are deeply committed to your privacy. Flow is designed as an offline-first application — meaning all your financial data (transactions, categories, budgets, debts) is stored locally on your device by default.

We do not operate servers that collect or process your personal financial data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

🔒 Flow contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics trackers, and no telemetry. Your spending habits remain entirely private.

03

Local Storage

Flow stores all your financial data in a local SQLite database on your device using the sqflite package. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to back it up to Google Drive.

App preferences (your name, chosen currency, theme, spending limits, and security settings) are stored using shared_preferences, which is local device storage equivalent to SharedPreferences on Android.

  • Your data is not transmitted to any server operated by Flow.
  • Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all locally stored data.
  • We recommend using the Google Drive backup feature before uninstalling.
  • Data can be exported as a JSON file from the Settings screen at any time.
04

Google Drive Backup

Flow offers an optional Google Drive backup feature. You must actively sign in with a Google account to enable this. It is entirely optional and off by default.

ℹ️ Flow only requests the restricted drive.appdata scope. This gives the app access to a private, app-specific folder inside your Google Drive that no other app or person can see — not even through the Google Drive web interface.

When you connect Google Drive:

  • Flow reads your basic Google profile (name, email, avatar) to display in the app.
  • Your transaction database is serialized and saved as flow_config.json inside your Drive's hidden appDataFolder.
  • This file is accessible only to Flow. No third party, including Google's standard Drive UI, can browse it.
  • You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from the Settings screen. Doing so revokes our access token.
  • Your Google account credentials are managed entirely by Google's Sign-In SDK — Flow never sees your password.

Google's handling of your account data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

05

Notifications

Flow may request permission to send you local push notifications. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on-device — no notification service, push server, or third-party messaging platform is involved.

Notifications may include reminders for:

  • Upcoming or overdue debt payment due dates.
  • Recurring transactions that are scheduled to be logged.
  • Daily or monthly spending limit warnings (if configured).

You can manage or disable notifications at any time through your device's system notification settings or from within the Flow app.

06

App Lock & Biometrics

Flow offers an optional App Lock feature to protect access to your financial data. You can secure the app with a PIN and optionally enable biometric authentication (fingerprint or face recognition).

  • Biometric data (fingerprints, facial geometry) is never accessed, stored, or transmitted by Flow.
  • Authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system via the local_auth API — the secure enclave on your phone.
  • Flow only receives a boolean response: authenticated or not.
  • Your PIN (if set) is stored in local device storage and never leaves your device.
🛡️ Enabling App Lock is strongly recommended if you share your device with others or carry sensitive financial data.

08

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, trade, or disclose your personal data to any third parties, with the following narrow exceptions:

  • Google Drive backup (your choice): If you opt in, a backup file is stored in your own Google Drive account, governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Manual export (your choice): If you use the JSON export feature, you control where that file goes (e.g. emailing it to yourself, saving to cloud storage).
  • Legal obligation: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or other legal process — though given our offline-first architecture, we hold no user data on any server to disclose.
09

Your Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control at all times:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the app at any time.
  • Export: Export a full copy of your data as JSON from Settings → Export Data.
  • Delete: Delete individual transactions within the app, or uninstall the app to remove all data from your device.
  • Google Drive data: Sign out from Google Drive in Settings to revoke access. You can also manually delete the backup file from your Google Drive app data.
  • Portability: Your exported JSON file is a human-readable format you can use independently of Flow.

If you are in the EU/EEA, you also have rights under the GDPR including rectification, restriction of processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Please contact us (see below) to exercise these rights.

10

Children's Privacy

Flow is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through the Google Drive backup feature, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.
